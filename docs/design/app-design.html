AMN Drift

A drifting sky behind every screen, made from the 28 widget skies. Frosted glass sits on top, and the accent colour comes from the sky. This page holds the numbers; the motion itself lives in the app, in packages/amn_ui.

Decisions

Every screen uses the skyHome shows it in full. Pages with a purpose (widget edit pages, the lab) mute it so they're easy to focus on.
Icon: DriftThree screen-blended orbs on a night gradient, plus a monochrome themed icon.
Icons: Fluent UI System Iconsfluentui_system_icons, 24 regular by default and filled for emphasis. No Material icons.
Grain breathes, never movesA fixed noise texture whose strength fades in and out on an uneven rhythm.
Fragment shader, not flutter_gpuflutter_gpu is on stable only behind a flag, is still a preview, and has an open Android startup deadlock (flutter#193890). A runtime fragment shader covers a 2D sky.
Greeting without a name"Good evening", from the phone's clock.

On the phone

Release build on a Galaxy A55, dark mode.

The sky

Four layers each walk through the 28 skies on their own clock. The clocks share no common beat, and a seeded hash picks each next sky (never the one being left), so the sky never visibly loops. It starts somewhere new on each launch.

Crossfade

Smoothstep between this sky and the next over the whole period, so a layer is always changing.

Orbits

Each glow's x and y are two sines with unrelated periods (19–76 s). Its radius breathes between 0.42 and 0.68 of the long side.

Tilt

Accelerometer delta from a slowly adapting rest pose, clamped to ±1. Offset = tilt × 0.07 × depth × screen, eased in at about 5 per second.

Cost

One full-screen shader pass at 30 fps from a timer, paused when the app is hidden. Dithered so the gradients don't band. Reduce motion freezes the sky and the tilt.

Glass

Glass: blur σ 22 with 150% saturation (one shared backdrop group), then tint, grain, a 1 px edge and a top highlight that fades out by a third of the height (max 40 px). Turn the blur off for glass that only sits on the sky, where it can't be seen.

Grain

A 96 px grey noise tile made once at startup, shown at 72 logical px with overlay blend. It never moves.

Breathing

strength = 0.15 + 0.85 × mix of sin(t / 7.3 s) at 0.6 and sin(t / 11.9 s) at 0.4. It fades in and out unevenly, from barely there to fully there. Fixed at 0.6 with reduce motion.

Accent and type

Accent from the sky

Near glow's top colour → HSL, saturation at least 0.55, lightness 0.40 (light) or 0.72 (dark). Text on it is #FFFFFF or #11131C. It updates only when the hue moves 14° or more, and the theme blends over 1.6 s.

Page transitions

Pages are see-through, so they fade instead of sliding: the new page fades in and rises 2.5% (420 ms, ease-out cubic) while the old one fades out in the first 60%.

App icon

An adaptive icon. All the art is in the background layer (the orbs are screen-blended onto the gradient), drawn by tool/make_icon.py at every density. The monochrome layer is three 55% discs inside the 66 dp safe circle.
